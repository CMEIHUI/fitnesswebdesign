Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my nearest New Era Fitness location?

Please visit our Find a Gym page to find a convenient location near you. We have over 5,000 locations worldwide, so there's a good chance we're just around the corner!

There are no New Era Fitness locations near me! Are you going to open one in my town?

We're always on the lookout for new communities to serve! Any locations that are coming soon are listed on our Find a Gym page. If you or someone you know is interested in opening a gym, you can learn more about franchise opportunities.

I heard that there's a New Era Fitness opening near me. When will it open?

Opening dates are subject to change, but you can find the most up-to-date information on the gym's specific page via our Find a Gym locator. You can also sign up for email alerts for that location.

How do I provide feedback about my membership experience?

We'd love to hear from you! Please contact your home gym directly to provide feedback about your experience. Alternatively, you can use the "Contact Us" form on our website to send a message to our corporate team.

How much does it cost for a membership?

Each New Era Fitness location is independently owned, so membership pricing may vary. Please check our Memberships page for general pricing tiers or contact your local gym for specific details.

How do I cancel my membership?

Membership cancellation policies are handled by your home gym. Please reach out to them directly for assistance with the cancellation process. Typically, a 30-day written notice is required.
